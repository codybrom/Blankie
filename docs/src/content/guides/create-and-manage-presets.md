A preset in Blankie is just a mix of sounds with their volumes saved together. Rain for focus, ocean for sleep, a café for work. One click and you're in a different one. Every preset can also carry its own artwork and accent color, so the moment you switch mixes the app looks the part too.

## Create a preset

1. Click the {{icon:plus|Add}} button at the top of the sidebar (Mac and iPad) or in your Library (iPhone) and choose **New Preset**.
2. Give it a name. You can add a creator name and artwork too, if you want.
3. Tap **Choose Sounds** to pick what goes in it.
4. Click **Create**.

> [!TIP]
> Already have something good going on the default preset? Hit its **Edit** button and Blankie will offer to spin up a new preset from whatever you've got playing.

## Edits save automatically

When a preset is active, anything you change (flipping sounds on or off, nudging volumes) gets saved to it right away. No save button to forget.

## Rename, edit, or delete a preset

Right-click a preset on Mac, or touch and hold on iPhone and iPad, then choose **Edit Preset** or **Delete Preset**. The edit sheet handles the name, sounds, artwork and theme, all covered below.

The built-in default preset can't be renamed or deleted, but it'll still take custom artwork and theme settings.

## Add artwork

Open the preset's edit sheet (right-click → **Edit Preset** on Mac, touch and hold on iPhone and iPad) and use the **Artwork** row to pick an image.

That image follows the preset wherever it goes:

- The **Now Playing** screen and your **lock screen** while the preset is playing
- The preset's thumbnail in your Library
- **CarPlay**, where artwork makes a preset easy to spot at a glance

On iPhone and iPad you can choose **animated artwork** instead, a gently moving scene that plays on your lock screen in place of a still image.

## Customize Theme

The **Customize Theme** section takes over from your default theme whenever the preset is active:

- **Accent Color**: a color that tints the whole app while this preset plays (all platforms)
- **View Mode**: lock the preset to a Grid or List layout (iPhone and iPad)
- **Background Blur**: decide whether the artwork backdrop is blurred (iPhone and iPad)

Leave a toggle off and the preset just follows your app-wide settings.

## Favorites

Tap the **star** {{icon:star}} on any preset row to drop it into the **Favorites** section, which lives at the top of your Library. You can reorder favorites in Edit mode, and they're also the presets that come up first in [CarPlay](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/intro-to-carplay-iphf33a514c9/ios).
