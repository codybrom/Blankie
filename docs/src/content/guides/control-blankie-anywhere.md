Blankie hooks into the system's Now Playing controls. Once your soundscape is going, you rarely need the app in front of you.

## On Mac

- **Media keys**: the {{kbdicon:play-pause}} play/pause key on your keyboard controls Blankie like any music app. The {{kbdicon:rewind}} and {{kbdicon:fast-forward}} keys will also let you navigate through sounds and presets you've marked as favorites.
- **Menu bar**: the [Now Playing item in the macOS menu bar](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/quickly-change-settings-mchl50f94f8f/mac) shows your active preset, with play/pause right there.
- **Controls menu**: inside the app, the Controls menu plays and pauses, steps through your favorites, and adjusts volume. The spacebar plays or pauses your whole mix too. The full list lives in [Blankie Keyboard Shortcuts for Mac](/guides/keyboard-shortcuts-mac/).

## On iPhone and iPad

- **Lock screen**: your preset's name and [artwork](/guides/create-and-manage-presets/) show up with playback controls, the same as a music app.
- **Control Center**: the [Now Playing tile](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-music-player-controls-iph676daac9b/ios) plays and pauses your mix from anywhere.

## Home Screen widgets on iPhone and iPad

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, open the widget gallery, and search for **Blankie**. Choose from four widgets:

| Widget | What it does | Sizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Now Playing** | Shows the current sound or mix, with play/pause controls | Small |
| **Favorites** | Plays your starred presets and sounds | Medium, large, XL (iOS 27) |
| **Pinned Sound** | Keeps one preset or sound ready to play | Small |
| **Quick Mix** | Toggles your Quick Mix sounds on and off | Medium, large, XL (iOS 27) |

After adding **Pinned Sound**, touch and hold it, choose **Edit Widget**, and select a preset or sound. For Favorites, star the items you want in Blankie; for Quick Mix, choose your Quick Mix sounds in the app.

For Favorites and Quick Mix, touch and hold the widget and choose **Edit Widget** to set its **Color**. Choose **Default** to use the accent saved in Blankie's Settings, or choose a named color. Each widget keeps its own choice. iOS applies your Home Screen's Tinted or Clear treatment when enabled.

Pinned Sound and Now Playing use the artwork and accent of the preset or sound they display.

On iOS 27, the portrait **XL Favorites** widget shows up to 12 favorites with names. **XL Quick Mix** gives each sound its own row with volume controls. Tap a sound to enter Quick Mix, then use **−** and **+** to adjust its level in five-percentage-point steps. Adjusting volume keeps playback paused if it was paused, and leaves your saved preset unchanged.

## Siri on iPhone and iPad

Use these English commands, then give Siri the name when asked:

- **Play a preset:** say "Start a preset in Blankie." When Siri asks which preset, say its name, such as "Lake Nights."
- **Play a sound:** say "Start a sound in Blankie." When Siri asks which sound, say its name, such as "Rain."

**Play Sound** plays the named sound on its own in solo mode, just like choosing a sound from Favorites. Your saved preset stays unchanged. **Play Preset** switches to the preset you name.

For a sleep timer, say "Start a sleep timer in Blankie," then give the duration when asked. To pause or resume playback, use the Lock Screen, Control Center or a Blankie widget. You can also create a shortcut with Blankie's Play or Pause action.

The [sleep timer guide](/guides/sleep-timer/) covers the timer actions.

## Shortcuts on Mac, iPhone and iPad

In the **Shortcuts** app, add an action and search for **Blankie**. Actions include Play, Pause, Toggle Playback, Play Preset, Play Sound, Start/Stop Sleep Timer, Set Volume and Set Sound Volume. Choose a preset or sound in the action's picker, then combine it with other steps in your shortcut.

On iPhone and iPad, **Set Volume** requires **Mix with Other Audio** to be enabled in Blankie's Settings. Otherwise, use your device's volume buttons. **Set Sound Volume** adjusts an individual sound's mix level.

## Spotlight on Mac, iPhone and iPad

Search Spotlight for the name of a preset or sound to find Blankie results. Sounds marked for preset use only are excluded. To start a specific preset or sound, use its widget, Siri phrase or Shortcuts action.

## From your headphones

[AirPods and other headphones with playback controls](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/use-controls-and-gestures-with-your-airpods-devb2c431317/web) do what you'd expect. Press to pause your soundscape, press again to pick it back up. Pausing keeps the whole mix intact, so every sound and volume returns exactly where you left it.

## From the car

Blankie has a full CarPlay app for switching presets and sounds from the dashboard. See [Blankie on CarPlay](/guides/blankie-carplay/).

## Why pause instead of stop?

Pausing never clears your mix. Whatever you had going (a three-sound rain blend at volumes you tuned by hand) is one tap from playing again.
