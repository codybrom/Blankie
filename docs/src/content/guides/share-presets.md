Mixed something worth passing along? Blankie saves any preset you've made as a single **.blankie** file. AirDrop it, message it or email it to anyone else running Blankie.

## What's inside a .blankie file

Everything it takes to rebuild your preset, exactly as you have it:

- The preset's sounds, volume levels, name and artwork
- Any **custom sounds** the preset uses. The actual audio files ride along too, with their icons and settings, so whoever opens it hears what you hear.

## Export a preset

1. Right-click the preset on Mac (or touch and hold on iPhone and iPad) and choose **Edit Preset**.
2. Click the **share** {{icon:share}} button in the toolbar.
3. Send it however you like: [AirDrop](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-airdrop-to-send-items-to-nearby-devices-iphcd8b9f0af/ios), Messages, Mail or just save the file for later.

You can share any custom preset you build.

## Import a preset someone sent you

- **On iPhone or iPad:** tap the .blankie file (from AirDrop, Messages or the [Files app](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/files-basics-iphe9d46e90f/ios)) and open it with Blankie. Or use {{icon:plus|Add}} **→ Import** in your Library and pick the file yourself.
- **On Mac:** choose **File → Import**, or press {{kbd:⌘ I}}, then select the .blankie file.

Blankie drops the preset into your Library and switches right to it. Any custom sounds tucked inside install on their own. If you already have a preset with the same name, the new one will get a slight rename so nothing gets overwritten.
