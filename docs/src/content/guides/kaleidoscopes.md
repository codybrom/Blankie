Kaleidoscopes are known for making beautiful, constantly changing patterns with the help of mirrors and colorful materials. The Kaleidoscopes in Blankie take inspiration from this idea, and create ever-changing soundscapes that evolve as you listen.

{{video:kaleidoscope-iphone}}

## What you're hearing

In Blankie, a Kaleidoscope is made of more than 130 tonal layers, ranging from low hums to soft shimmers, and everything in between brightening and fading all on its own. Because the layers are always moving independently, there's no loop to notice and you never hear exactly the same combination twice.

Blankie's Kaleidoscopes are slow, soft and musical. They're also immersive and evolving. While not explicitly spatial, the sounds are programmed to move binaurally so that you perceive them as coming from different directions. The best way to experience the full effect is with headphones, but they can still be enjoyed on speakers.

{{audio:radiance}}

The 16 Kaleidoscopes available now are *Air*, *Beginnings*, *Dawn*, *Depth*, *Dreaming*, *Dusk*, *Ease*, *Gentleness*, *Hush*, *Lull*, *Mist*, *Radiance*, *Stillness*, *Undertone*, *Velvet* and *Warmth*.

## Play a Kaleidoscope

- **iPhone:** Back out to your Library and tap the **Kaleidoscopes** tab, then tap one.
- **iPad and Mac:** select **Kaleidoscopes** in the sidebar, then choose one from the list.
- **CarPlay:** You can find Kaleidoscopes mixed in with other sounds in the **Sounds** tab, but they won't have the animated visual effect.

You can also find any of them by name in the **Search** tab on iPhone. Other features like the sleep timer, AirPlay and media controls all work the way they do for other sounds.

## Play with the visual (iPhone, iPad and Mac)

When you play a Kaleidoscope on its own, a visual kaleidoscope fills the player background and moves in time with the sound.

- **Drag** anywhere on it to move the visual yourself. Let go and it settles back into the sound's own pace.
- **On iPhone**, tap the visual to hide the controls and tap again to bring them back.
- **On Mac**, go full screen to give the visual the whole display. It also shows up in the menu bar mixer.
- **On iPhone Duo**, the visual covers both pages when the phone is open.

{{video:kaleidoscope-iphone-hidden}}

Each Kaleidoscope has its own shapes and colors, and that same picture is its artwork on the Lock Screen, in Control Center and in CarPlay.

## Mix Kaleidoscopes with other sounds

Playing a Kaleidoscope on its own? Tap **+** to add another sound and hear them together. For example, *Gentleness* and *Forest* mix really well together. Save the mix as a [preset](/guides/create-and-manage-presets/), or just let it go when you're done.

A Kaleidoscope, like every other sound, can also be added to any of your presets with two things to keep in mind:

- Kaleidoscopes count as music. To prevent unintended overlap Blankie only allows one music sound at a time. Turn on a second one and the first turns off.
- If you create a [Spatial Audio](/guides/spatial-audio-mixing/) mix, a Kaleidoscope can't be placed spatially and will remain in plain stereo.

Also, keep in mind that the visual kaleidoscope only appears when a Kaleidoscope is playing by itself. In a mix, you'll see the preset's artwork.

## Where the sounds come from

Fifteen of Blankie's Kaleidoscopes were remixed from ambient recordings by Matio888 on Freesound. These recordings are shared under a Creative Commons Attribution license. *Beginnings* is built from original recordings made for Blankie. You can find links to the original recordings in a Kaleidoscope's sound settings under the **Remixed From** heading, or find them linked on the Credits page.
